Recovering lithium and cobalt without strong acids

My M.Sc. thesis: a mechanochemical route for spent Li-ion cathodes using a food-grade natural deep eutectic solvent at room temperature, validated on real Al-contaminated industrial NMC and LCO scrap rather than synthetic powders.

  • Led 6 experimental campaigns (~30 trials) to map leaching kinetics and optimal molar ratios.

  • Characterized feedstock and products by NMR, XRD, XRF, ICP-OES, SEM, EDS and UV-Vis. Manuscript in preparation.
